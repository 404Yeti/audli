# Conversational feedback fix

The V0.2 evaluator's generic `feedback` field flowed directly into final TTS, with an adaptation sentence appended. Its schema bounded characters, not spoken length or conversational intent. A question in that field bypassed the otherwise bounded interactive question workflow.

V0.2.1 keeps evaluator notes in the persisted evidence assessment, but constructs a separate terminal message from final evidence and the deterministic adaptation event. It can acknowledge one observation, correct one confirmed contradiction using the passage's expected information, and state what happens next. Insufficient evidence never supplies a correction. Questions remain in the existing interactive follow-up turn, with the existing two-question maximum and one clarification per information unit. Remaining uncertainty remains unknown and keeps difficulty stable.

The complete final message, including its transition, is validated for at most three sentences and `AUDLI_MAX_FEEDBACK_WORDS` words (default 50, configurable 30–50), with no questions, evidence requests, percentages or numerical scores. Evaluator prose is never a candidate for terminal speech: even malformed question/report copy cannot reach it. Unsafe or oversized passage-specific corrections use a complete shorter observation instead of truncating generated prose. This intentionally uses bounded application-owned copy rather than another model call. Scores and evidence remain available under See details.

Old cached final audio is bypassed using a versioned key and a digest of the safe terminal text. Already-completed V0.2 assessments also receive the safe spoken message without changing their scores, progress or adaptation. TTS remains independently retryable. The button that generates a new adaptive exercise now says **Keep going**.

## Chrome acceptance checklist

Restart the backend/frontend and hard-refresh Chrome before testing.

1. Summarize all important information, including contextual vocabulary and reasons. Expect no unnecessary question and short spoken feedback without scores.
2. Omit a relevant vocabulary meaning or inference. Expect one actual spoken question, a recording turn, and a response before final feedback. Transcript must still be inaccessible during that question.
3. Explicitly give a wrong location/time/detail. Expect a correction, not a rescue question for the confirmed contradiction. Compare with omitting the same information: omission must remain unknown rather than wrong.
4. Leave multiple dimensions uncertain, including after answering. Expect no more than two follow-ups, no third question hidden in feedback, unknown evidence under See details, and unchanged difficulty.
5. Listen to the entire closing message: no question, “try to find,” request to remember more, scores, or long report. It should have one to three short sentences, at most 50 words including the next step.
6. Choose Keep going. Confirm it creates a new exercise using the final adaptation. It must not replay the previous clip. Show transcript should work only after completion.
7. Retry failed feedback audio or reload a completed exercise from before this fix. Expect safe concise text/audio, with the assessment and progress preserved and no duplicate adaptation.

Automated fixtures test workflow and safety, not real-model semantic accuracy. Real-provider service tests use synthetic speech and do not constitute Chrome verification. No Chrome verification was performed for this patch.
